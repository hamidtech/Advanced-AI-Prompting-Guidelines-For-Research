## Maintenance

Before changing a technical claim, open its current official source and update the date and observed scope. A link is not proof of verification. When retrieval fails, qualify the claim or leave verification pending. This register does not claim access to commercial full texts or every disciplinary database. No commercial model API, subscription or signed-in research service was tested in this pass.
